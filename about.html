Company Overview

A Legacy of
Legal Excellence.

Founded in 1998, Sterling & Associates has grown from a boutique Manhattan practice into a global law firm with 50+ attorneys across 14 countries. We combine the precision of a white-shoe firm with the agility of a modern legal practice.

Our clients include Fortune 500 corporations, emerging growth companies, financial institutions, and high-net-worth individuals. We are consistently ranked among the top corporate law firms by Chambers & Partners and The Legal 500.

Our Journey

Firm History

From a two-attorney partnership to a global legal powerhouse — our story is one of relentless growth and unwavering commitment.

1998
Founded in New York

James Sterling and Margaret Chen establish the firm with a vision to provide premium corporate counsel with a personal touch.

2003
First International Office

Opened our London office to serve growing cross-border client needs, expanding into UK and EU markets.

2008
M&A Practice Launch

Formalized our Mergers & Acquisitions practice, which quickly became a top-tier practice by deal volume.

2012
Asia-Pacific Expansion

Opened offices in Singapore and Hong Kong, establishing a strong presence across APAC markets.

2018
50 Attorneys Milestone

Reached 50 attorneys firm-wide and recognized as a top corporate law firm by Chambers & Partners.

2024
Global Leadership

Expanded to 9 offices across 14 countries, serving over 500 corporate clients with 50+ specialized attorneys.
