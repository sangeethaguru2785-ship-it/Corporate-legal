Frequently Asked Questions

Common answers to help you get started

You can book a consultation from the My Appointments page. Choose your preferred counsel, select a time slot, and confirm. You’ll receive a confirmation notice in the portal and by email.

For most matters, bring any relevant contracts, correspondence, and identification. Your counsel will confirm exactly what is needed after booking, and you can upload files securely in advance from the Documents page.

Invoices are issued for each completed engagement and appear in Payment History. You can pay online by card, and a receipt is generated automatically. Outstanding balances are shown at the top of the Payment History page.

Yes. Appointments can be rescheduled or cancelled from the My Appointments page up to 24 hours before the start time. Fees may apply for late cancellations, which will be reflected on your invoice.

Use the “Share a document” form on the Documents page. Files are transferred over an encrypted channel and your counsel receives a secure, access-controlled link. All uploads are logged in your document history.
