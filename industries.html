Deep Industry Knowledge

We don't just know the law — we know your industry. Our attorneys bring specialized sector experience that translates into pragmatic, commercially aware legal advice.

Information Technology

From SaaS agreements and software licensing to data privacy and cybersecurity, we advise tech companies from seed-stage startups to global platforms on their most critical legal matters.

Manufacturing

Supply chain contracts, regulatory compliance, product liability defense, environmental regulations, and international trade law for industrial and consumer goods manufacturers.

Healthcare

Healthcare regulatory compliance, HIPAA privacy, life sciences IP, clinical trial agreements, FDA regulatory counsel, and healthcare M&A for providers, payers, and innovators.

Banking & Finance

Financial services regulation, fintech licensing, securities law compliance, fund formation, private equity transactions, and debt financing for banks, funds, and financial innovators.

Real Estate

Commercial leasing, property acquisitions and dispositions, real estate development, REIT formation and compliance, land use, zoning, and construction law across all property classes.

Startups

Entity formation, founder agreements, venture capital financing, equity incentive plans, IP strategy, and exit planning. We help founders build from the ground up with a solid legal foundation.

E-Commerce

Terms of service, privacy policies, marketplace compliance, consumer protection, payment processing agreements, cross-border trade, and digital advertising law for online businesses.

Energy

Renewable energy project development, oil and gas regulatory compliance, energy trading agreements, environmental impact assessment, and energy sector M&A and joint ventures.
