How We Hire

Our Hiring Process

A transparent, collaborative process designed to help you put your best foot forward and get to know the firm.

Step 01
Apply Online

Submit your resume and a brief cover letter through our careers portal. Applications are reviewed by our recruiting team every week.

Step 02
Screening & Assessment

A friendly recruiter call followed by a focused written or case assessment where it's relevant to the role.

Step 03
Team Interviews

Meet partners, associates, and peers across three to four structured interviews that give you a real feel for the work and culture.

Step 04
Offer & Negotiation

Successful candidates receive a detailed written offer with full transparency on compensation, benefits, and growth path.

Step 05
Onboarding

A structured 90-day onboarding with a dedicated mentor, firm-wide orientation, and access to our learning platform from day one.
